About Us

A chapter, and a home

GNASS CCTU exists to help Adventist students grow spiritually, academically and socially during their time on campus. We meet weekly, serve our wider campus community, and walk with students from freshers' week to graduation.

Chapter dues and offerings keep our ministries running — see the Give section below for how to support us.
